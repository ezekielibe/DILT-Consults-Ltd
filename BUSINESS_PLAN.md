# DILT Consults Ltd Business Plan Draft

## 1. Executive Summary
DILT Consults Ltd is a technology consulting and implementation company focused on helping organizations transform operations and decision-making through practical, secure, and scalable digital solutions.

DILT stands for Digital Innovation and Leveraged Technology. The company combines strategic consulting with hands-on implementation across artificial intelligence, Microsoft Copilot solutions, workflow automation, and cloud-enabled platforms. DILT Consults Ltd is built to support clients that want measurable business outcomes from technology, not just software adoption.

The company operates through three service pillars:
- AI and Copilot Solutions
- Digital Transformation and Automation
- Technology Consulting, Licensing and Implementation

By aligning business priorities with fit-for-purpose technologies, DILT Consults Ltd aims to deliver productivity gains, better decision quality, stronger governance, and sustainable long-term value.

DILT Consults Ltd promotes responsible technology adoption through practical governance frameworks tailored to each client context.

### Governance Commitments
- Data protection and confidentiality by default
- Role-based access and least-privilege principles in solution design
- Traceability for automated workflows and AI-assisted outputs

### Responsible AI Orientation
- Transparent communication of AI capabilities and limitations
- Bias, risk, and quality checks in implementation workflows
- Incremental rollout with clear success criteria and controls

- Establish delivery governance and quality controls

### Days 31 to 60: Pilot Delivery and Proof Points

## 14. Risk Awareness and Mitigation Focus
### Key Risk Areas
- Technology adoption resistance within client teams
- Scope creep in transformation initiatives
- Governance and compliance gaps during rapid rollout
- Over-customization that impacts maintainability

### Mitigation Approach
- Early stakeholder alignment and change communication
- Phased delivery with clear scope boundaries
- Governance checkpoints embedded in project lifecycle
- Preference for scalable, supportable design patterns
